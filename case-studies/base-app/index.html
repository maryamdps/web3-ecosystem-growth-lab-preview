01 / PRODUCT & AUDIENCE RESEARCH

Understand the product before marketing it.

First establish what the product does, what jobs it may serve and which people have a reason to care. The segments below are proposed audiences to investigate — not claims about Base's actual user demographics.

PRODUCT SNAPSHOT

What is Base App?

Base App brings together several onchain experiences, including social discovery, trading, payments and app discovery. Rather than treating it as a single-purpose wallet, the GTM question is how to make its range of capabilities feel useful and understandable to different people.

MARKETING IMPLICATION

One app does not mean one audience.

A person looking to receive a payment has a different motivation from a creator looking to build a community or a crypto user exploring digital assets. Each needs a different entry message and first action.

AUDIENCE 01 · MONEY MOVERS

People with cross-border payment needs

Freelancers paid by overseas clients, remote workers and people who regularly send or receive money internationally.

Need: Understand whether a stablecoin payment could be a practical option, including fees, conversion and local availability.
AUDIENCE 02 · CREATORS & COMMUNITIES

People building or participating in online communities

Creators, community leaders and audiences interested in social discovery and new digital participation models.

Need: Find relevant creators, connect with audiences and understand what participation enables.
AUDIENCE 03 · MARKET EXPLORERS

People already curious about digital assets

Crypto-aware users who want to discover assets, follow activity and explore onchain applications.

Need: Access clear information, understand risks and make informed decisions.
Research questions to validate

Which audience has the strongest unmet need? Which product features are available in the target market? What is the current user journey? What are the biggest trust, setup, fee or usability barriers? What existing alternatives do people use?

02 / GROWTH OPPORTUNITY

Find the gap between product capability and user understanding.

Research becomes useful when it produces a specific growth problem to solve. The following is a strategic diagnosis to validate through product walkthroughs, user interviews and funnel data.

POTENTIAL FRICTION

Feature breadth can create choice overload.

When one app offers several different experiences, a new visitor may not know where to begin or which feature matches their need.

GROWTH OPPORTUNITY

Lead with one job-to-be-done.

Build separate entry journeys around clear motivations — for example, understanding a stablecoin payment, discovering creator communities or exploring digital assets.

ACQUISITION GAP

Generic messaging

“Explore Web3” may be too broad to connect with a real-life need.

ACTIVATION GAP

Unclear first action

Users may sign up or connect a wallet without completing the task that brought them in.

RETENTION GAP

No reason to return

After one interaction, users need a relevant next step tied to their original motivation.

Strategic growth question

How might Base App acquire users through a specific, relevant use case — then guide them to a successful first action and a reason to return?

What I would investigate before launch

Qualitative research

  • Interview people across the three proposed segments.
  • Walk through the app as a first-time user.
  • Identify confusing terms, trust concerns and missing information.

Quantitative analysis

  • Review acquisition source and landing-page conversion.
  • Map setup completion and first-value conversion.
  • Compare repeat-use cohorts by initial use case.
03 / GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY

One product. Different reasons to start.

Build a use-case-led acquisition strategy. Keep the core product positioning consistent, but tailor the message, channel, landing page and first action to each audience.

PROPOSED POSITIONINGYour starting point for the onchain economy.

Discover useful apps, connect with people and access onchain services through one guided experience.

Useful first

UNDERSTAND THE OPTIONS ↗
CREATIVE BRIEF

Campaign: Money Moves Beyond Borders

Audience: Freelancers and remote workers who receive payments from overseas clients.

Insight: They care about getting paid reliably. They do not necessarily care about blockchain technology.

Message: Start with the payment need. Explain stablecoins as one possible payment method, including trade-offs and limitations.

Creative execution:

  • Reel: “How does a stablecoin payment work?”
  • Carousel: sender → network → recipient, with fees and conversion explained.
  • Creator video: freelancer walks through a real payment workflow, where legally and practically supported.
  • Landing page: requirements, supported regions, fees, risks and step-by-step guide.
Sample content sequence
CONTENT 01 · EDUCATE

Stablecoins, explained simply.

A 30-second video explaining what a stablecoin is, how it differs from a bank transfer and what it does not guarantee.

CONTENT 02 · DEMONSTRATE

Follow the payment journey.

A visual walkthrough showing the sender, wallet, network confirmation and recipient — with clear fee and conversion caveats.

CONTENT 03 · CONVERT

Is this option right for you?

A practical checklist covering supported locations, wallet setup, recipient readiness, custody, conversion and risk.
