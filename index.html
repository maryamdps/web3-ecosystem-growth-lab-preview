01 / Project overview

Learning the ecosystem by experiencing it.

This project connects practical product exploration with the growth skills I have developed across marketing, community building, content and go-to-market work. The aim is to understand both the mechanics of on-chain products and the reasons people choose to use them — or drop off.

Research questions

  • What makes a first wallet or dApp interaction understandable and approachable?
  • Where do users encounter friction across discovery, connection and transaction flows?
  • What motivates users to return after their first on-chain action?
  • How can ecosystem campaigns create useful participation rather than one-off activity?

Intended outcome

A documented body of practical product observations, ecosystem research and a clearly labelled growth proposal. Findings will be supported by dated notes, screenshots and source links as the work is completed.

02 / Starting point

Establishing a clear baseline.

The project begins with foundational wallet familiarity and exchange experience. The baseline is intentionally specific: owning a wallet or purchasing crypto through an exchange is not the same as completing an on-chain transaction.

Current experience

MetaMask wallet created. Prior experience purchasing crypto through Binance.

Experience to develop

Verified on-chain interactions, dApp connections, transaction inspection, token approvals, swaps and cross-chain concepts.

Baseline reflects experience confirmed at project start. Additional activity will only be added after it has been completed and documented.

04 / Working method

Learn it. Test it. Analyse it. Apply it.

A repeatable process keeps the work grounded in actual product use and observable evidence, then connects those findings to commercial and ecosystem growth decisions.

STEP 01

Explore

Use products and protocols in a controlled, low-risk way. Record the exact journey and context.

STEP 02

Document

Capture screenshots, transaction references where applicable, sources and dated observations.

STEP 03

Interpret

Identify user needs, friction points, ecosystem patterns and potential growth levers.

STEP 04

Recommend

Turn evidence into product, partnership, campaign and retention recommendations.

06 / Principles

Useful participation over vanity activity.

The project is designed to build practical fluency without confusing activity with expertise. It will prioritise responsible exploration, accurate interpretation and user-centred recommendations.

01

Evidence-led

Distinguish verified activity, research observations, hypotheses and proposed campaign ideas.

02

User-centred

Consider clarity, usability, trust and the reasons users may hesitate or disengage.

03

Risk-aware

Use controlled exploration, protect wallet credentials and avoid unnecessary financial exposure.
