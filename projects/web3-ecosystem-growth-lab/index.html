01 / Overview

Where technology meets adoption.

Web3 products introduce new concepts, behaviours and trust decisions for users. This project examines the infrastructure behind those experiences and considers how ecosystem teams can make complex products easier to understand, access and use.

Research focus

  • How blockchains record and validate activity
  • How Layer 1 networks and Layer 2 rollups work together
  • How wallets, bridges and decentralised applications fit into the ecosystem
  • Where education, onboarding and trust influence adoption

Strategic lens

Technical understanding is connected to the user journey: discovery, comprehension, first action and repeat participation. The focus is not activity for its own sake, but helping users recognise value and make informed decisions.

03 / Growth strategy

Turning understanding into participation.

The research informs a practical growth lens for Web3 products: reduce uncertainty at each stage of the journey, make the first meaningful action easier, and give users a reason to return.

01

Discovery

Reach relevant audiences through clear use cases, trusted education, creator partnerships and ecosystem communities.

Awareness · Qualified interest
02

Comprehension

Explain product value, wallet requirements, network choices, fees and risks in language users can act on.

Education · Confidence
03

Activation

Design a guided first-use journey around one useful action, with clear prompts and visible feedback.

Onboarding · First success
04

Retention

Build ongoing reasons to participate through product utility, community, relevant updates and progressive education.

Repeat use · Meaningful engagement

These are strategic recommendations derived from the research framework, not claims of an executed campaign or measured client results.
