03 / ADOPTION FRAMEWORK

From first touch to repeat use.

A practical lens for diagnosing friction across the user journey. The goal is not to maximise wallet connections or transaction counts, but to help the right users reach and recognise product value.

01 / DISCOVER

Earn attention

Make the use case relevant. Reach people through trusted communities, clear education and credible ecosystem voices.

Signal: qualified interest
02 / UNDERSTAND

Build confidence

Explain what the product does, what a wallet is needed for, what a transaction will do and what risks to consider.

Signal: informed intent
03 / ACTIVATE

Deliver first value

Help users complete one meaningful product action with clear guidance, useful feedback and minimal avoidable friction.

Signal: first value event
04 / RETURN

Create a reason to stay

Support repeat participation through recurring utility, reliable experiences, community and relevant product education.

Signal: repeat meaningful use
04 / RESEARCH METHOD

Grounded in sources. Clear about limits.

The lab separates established technical information from interpretation and strategic hypotheses. Recommendations are not presented as proven outcomes.

Evidence base

Research notes draw on protocol documentation, technical explainers and ecosystem materials. Sources are linked within each report so readers can trace key explanations.

How conclusions are framed

Technical descriptions, user-experience implications and growth hypotheses are distinguished. Where primary user research or product analytics have not been conducted, findings are presented as questions to validate — not measured facts.

05 / PRINCIPLES

Useful over noisy.

Web3 growth should be built around understanding, trust and product value — not hype or activity metrics in isolation.

Explain the mechanism.

Make the technology understandable without flattening important technical differences.

Respect the user.

Make fees, permissions, custody and transaction consequences easier to understand.

Measure meaningful use.

Look beyond impressions and raw transactions to activation, repeat use and retained users.
