01 / THE ECOSYSTEM

A blockchain is infrastructure.
The ecosystem is everything built around it.

People rarely come for the underlying protocol. They come to do something useful, interesting or valuable.

01 · Foundation

Network

Shared infrastructure that records and verifies activity.

02 · Access

Wallet

The tool people use to manage keys, assets and on-chain interactions.

03 · Utility

Applications

Products powered by smart contracts — from finance to games and identity.

02 / SCALING

Layer 1 and Layer 2, in simple terms.

Different layers can work together to support activity, while making different trade-offs.

LAYER 1

Ethereum

The base network: it provides core security and settlement for activity on its ecosystem.

LAYER 2

Base · Arbitrum · ZKsync

Separate execution environments that process transactions and use Ethereum for settlement or security, depending on their design.

Think of it this way: Ethereum is the shared railway foundation. Layer 2s are different metro systems connected to it. They are not all identical, and their security models still need to be understood.
03 / THE GROWTH CHALLENGE

Attention is not adoption.

Growth happens when people understand the value, complete a meaningful first action and have a reason to return.

01 / DISCOVER

Find it

The product reaches the right audience.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Trust it

People understand what it does and what to expect.

03 / ACTIVATE

Try it

They complete a useful first action.

04 / RETURN

Value it

There is a genuine reason to come back.

04 / STRATEGIC HYPOTHESES

Three places a growth team can start.

These are hypotheses to test — not campaigns launched or results achieved.

Make discovery useful

Organise entry points around what people want to do, not protocol jargon.

Design for first success

Reduce friction in wallet setup, network choice, fees and the first task.

Build a reason to return

Connect the product to recurring utility, not one-off incentives.
